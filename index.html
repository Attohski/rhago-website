Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your application.

How long does the application process take?
Typically 4-8 weeks from submitting your documents to receiving an admission letter. Some universities respond faster, especially for language programs. We track every application and keep you updated.
What documents do I need to provide?
For degree programs: passport biodata page, certificates, transcripts, bank statement, passport photo, non-criminal record, CV, physical examination form, 2 recommendation letters, English proficiency proof, research proposal, and a short intro video. For Chinese language programs, the list is shorter — just the essentials.
Do you help with scholarships?
Yes. We help you identify and apply for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC), Confucius Institute Scholarship, and university-specific scholarships. Our team guides you through the essays and documentation.
Is my personal information secure?
Absolutely. All documents are stored securely and encrypted. Only you and our application team can access your file. We never share your information with third parties.
What if I don't get admitted?
We apply to multiple universities on your behalf to maximize your chances. In the rare case that no admission is received, we work with you on a revised strategy for the next intake at no additional cost.
